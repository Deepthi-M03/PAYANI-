# Data and asset attribution

## Railway data (ADR 002)

| Dataset | Type | Source | Licence / terms | Snapshot | Shown as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stations (35) | Real station codes and names; coordinates rounded to 2 decimals | Hand-compiled public facts | Facts, not a copyrighted dataset | 2026-11-26 | Simulated (part of the synthetic network) |
| Trains and timetable (96 trains) | Fully synthetic, generated by `payani/domain/catalog_synth.py` (seed 2026) | PAYANI | Original work | 2026-11-26 | Simulated |
| Booking caps (GN 6, TQ 4) | Policy values | GN: IIT Kanpur SATHEE guide (secondary); TQ: ixigo and others | Cited per row in `booking_policies` | Checked 2026-11-26 | Shown with source and date |
| Demand data | Fully synthetic, `payani/domain/forecasting.py` (seed 7) | PAYANI | Original work | — | Simulated |

No open timetable dataset is imported yet. Before one is added, its licence and usage terms are read and recorded here, and anything unclear is not imported.
